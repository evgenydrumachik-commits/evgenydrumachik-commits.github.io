Why the early years vanish on a normal chart

RAM grew multiplicatively. A log scale gives each doubling similar visual space, so 2–64 MB remains readable next to 16–64 GB.

What “normal range” means

The bands represent common mainstream configurations, not the cheapest possible PC or extreme workstation configurations.

Laptops caught up

Portable PCs began with a clear memory disadvantage. By the 2010s, laptop and desktop mainstream configurations had converged substantially.

Method & caveat

This is an educational reconstruction, not a worldwide sales census. No consistent public database reports a true annual global average RAM for every laptop and desktop sold since 1990. The center lines model representative mainstream configurations; yearly values between anchor years are geometrically interpolated. “Combined” is the midpoint of the two modeled centers, not shipment-weighted.

Anchors were cross-checked against period PC references, Windows hardware requirements and current manufacturer configurations: DOS Days 1990, DOS Days 1995, Windows Vista guide, Windows 10 requirements, Windows 11 requirements, and current MacBook Air specs.
